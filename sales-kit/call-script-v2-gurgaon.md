## ⚡ THE 3-MINUTE PRE-CALL RITUAL
*(Do this before EVERY call. Never skip it.)*

1. **Google:** "[Clinic Name] Gurgaon" → Note review count + rating
2. **Open their website** → Is there a booking button? Does it look modern? Works on mobile?
3. **Check WhatsApp** → Is it a personal number or WhatsApp Business?
4. **Note their zone:** Sector 29/32/40/45/47/48/49, DLF, Golf Course Rd, Sohna Rd, MG Road

**You now have 3 weapons to open with. Use exactly 1 per call.**

## 🚪 GETTING PAST THE RECEPTIONIST

### ❌ Wrong approach:
> "Can I speak to the doctor? It's regarding a business proposal."
*(She'll say "Doctor is busy, call back" — and you never get through)*

### ✅ Right approach:
> "Hi, good morning — is this [Clinic Name]? I'm [Name] calling from SmartDocSystem.
> Quick question for you actually — **are you the one who manages patient inquiries and bookings here?**"

**If she says YES:**
> "Perfect — actually you're exactly who I wanted to speak with first.
> We've been working with a few dental clinics in Gurgaon and we found something interesting about how patient inquiries get handled after clinic hours. I wanted to understand your situation before speaking to Dr. [Name].
> **What's the best 5-minute window to reach the doctor directly today?**"

**Why this works:** You made her feel important. She's now your ally, not your gatekeeper.

## 🎯 THE SCRIPT — THREE OPENING VARIANTS

**Pick the variant that matches your pre-call research. Never use all three on the same call.**

---

### VARIANT A — "The Google Test"
*Use when: Clinic has < 100 reviews OR their website is old/basic*

---

**[Doctor answers or you've been put through]**

> *"Dr. [Name], good morning. My name is [Name] from SmartDocSystem — I promise this will take 90 seconds and I think you'll find it interesting. Is this an okay moment?"*

**[Wait for yes]**

> *"I did a quick check before calling — I searched 'dental implants [their area/sector] Gurgaon' the way your patients would search at 9 PM. Your clinic doesn't appear in the first three results.*
>
> *I want to be clear — this has nothing to do with your clinic's quality. It's a visibility gap that's very common in Gurgaon. And the reason it matters is that 80% of patients click one of those first three results and never scroll further.*
>
> *The clinics that are filling those top slots are capturing patients who should be yours. I wanted to ask if that's something on your radar."*

**[Pause. Let them react.]**

---

### VARIANT B — "The After-Hours Leak"
*Use when: Clinic has no booking system / only a phone number on their website*

---

> *"Dr. [Name], good morning — [Name] from SmartDocSystem. 90 seconds of your time — I think you'll find this genuinely relevant. Okay?"*

**[Wait]**

> *"I want to ask you something that most clinic owners don't have an exact answer to — and it's costing them real revenue every week.*
>
> *In the last month, how many patient inquiries do you estimate came through WhatsApp or missed calls after 8 PM — when your clinic was closed? 10? 20? More?*
>
> *Most clinics in Gurgaon genuinely don't know. There's no system tracking it. And here's what the data shows — 70% of dental treatment searches in India happen after clinic hours. That means if someone in DLF or Sector 45 searches 'root canal near me' at 10 PM and sends your clinic a WhatsApp — if there's no automated response, they book the next clinic that replies.*
>
> *That's not a marketing problem. That's a leaking pipe."*

**[Pause. Let them sit with it.]**

---

### VARIANT C — "The Competitor Clock"
*Use when: You know a major competitor near them (Cosmodontist, AK Global Dent, or any branded multispecialty chain)*

---

> *"Dr. [Name], I'll be direct with you — my name is [Name] from SmartDocSystem. I called because I wanted to speak with you before you start feeling a pressure you may not have noticed yet. One minute of your time?"*

**[Wait]**

> *"There are 3 to 4 dental clinics within [1–2 km] of yours that recently set up AI-powered patient systems — automated WhatsApp replies, online booking, 24-hour inquiry capture.*
>
> *These aren't big corporate chains. They're clinics very similar to yours in size. But they've built a digital engine that's actively pulling patients off Google at 10 PM while your clinic is closed.*
>
> *I'm not calling to scare you. I'm calling because the window to build this advantage is maybe 6 months before it becomes the industry standard in Gurgaon — and I want to help the right clinics get there first."*

## 🔍 DISCOVERY QUESTIONS
*(Ask 2–3 max. One of these will hit the nerve. Watch for the pause — that's where the real conversation starts.)*

| Question | What You're Diagnosing |
|----------|----------------------|
| "Roughly how many WhatsApp or missed call inquiries do you get per week that don't convert into bookings?" | Lead leakage awareness |
| "If a patient searched 'dental implants [their sector]' at 10 PM on Google — do you think they'd find your clinic?" | Visibility gap |

| "Has the number of new patients slowed down compared to 12 months ago?" | Growth anxiety — most honest answer you'll get |
