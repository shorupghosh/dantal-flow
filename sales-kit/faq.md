- An **AI chatbot receptionist** that works 24/7 to answer patient questions and book appointments

**It's much more than a website.**

A regular website just shows your clinic information. DentalFlow AI is an active patient acquisition system. It:

- **Talks to patients** automatically through the AI chatbot
- **Captures their name and phone number** without any manual effort
- **Books appointments** directly from the website

- **Tracks all leads** in a dashboard so nothing gets missed

Most dental clinic websites are passive. DentalFlow AI is active — it works for your clinic 24 hours a day.

**Yes — if you are a dental clinic in India, this system is built for you.**

DentalFlow AI works especially well for:
- Clinics that get inquiries on WhatsApp or by phone and lose some leads
- Clinics that are closed in the evenings or on weekends and miss calls
- Clinics that want more patients without hiring extra staff

If patients are searching for dentists online and you're not showing up — or if they can't book easily — DentalFlow AI solves that.
