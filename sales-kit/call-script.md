# 📞 SmartDocSystem — Sales Call Script
### For: Sister / Sales Representative
### Product: DentalFlow AI — AI Growth System for Dental Clinics

---

> **HOW TO USE THIS SCRIPT**
> Read it naturally — don't rush. Speak slowly and clearly.
> You don't need to say every single word. This is a guide, not a robot script.
> If they interrupt, listen first. Answer their question, then continue.

---

## ─── BEFORE THE CALL ───

✅ Make sure you have the **demo link** ready: `https://dantal-flow.vercel.app/`
✅ Have the **pricing PDF** saved on your phone to share via WhatsApp
✅ Know their name and clinic name if possible before calling

---

## ─── STEP 1: OPENING (First 30 seconds) ───

> **Goal: Be polite, introduce yourself, and get permission to talk.**

**Say this:**

> *"Hello, am I speaking with [Name]? This is [Your Name] calling from SmartDocSystem. We help dental clinics in India get more patients using AI technology. I just need 2 minutes of your time — is now a good time to speak?"*

---

**If they say YES → Go to Step 2**

**If they say "call me later" →**

> *"Of course! What time works best for you? I'll call you at exactly that time."*
> *(Note the time. Call back at that exact time.)*

**If they say "I'm not interested" →**

> *"That's totally fine! Would it be okay if I just WhatsApp you one short message so you can see what we do? Many clinic owners found it very useful."*

---

## ─── STEP 2: DISCOVERY QUESTIONS (1–2 minutes) ───

> **Goal: Understand their situation and make them feel heard.**

**Say this:**

> *"Thank you for your time. I'd love to understand your clinic better first. Can I ask — do you currently have a website for your clinic?"*

**Listen carefully. Then follow up:**

---

**If NO website →**

> *"So right now, if a patient searches for a dentist online late at night, how do they find you?"*

**If YES website →**

> *"That's great. Are patients currently booking appointments through your website, or are they calling the clinic directly?"*

---

**Follow-up question (always ask this one):**

> *"How many new patient inquiries does your clinic get per month on WhatsApp or by phone? And how many of those actually book an appointment?"*

*(Let them answer. Most will say they lose many leads.)*

---

> *"I see. So you might be losing some patients simply because no one could respond in time — especially after hours or on weekends?"*

---

## ─── STEP 3: THE PITCH (60 seconds) ───

> **Goal: Explain DentalFlow AI clearly, in simple words.**

**Say this:**

> *"This is exactly the problem we solve at SmartDocSystem.*
>
> *We build what we call a DentalFlow AI System for dental clinics — it's basically a premium website for your clinic that works like a smart employee 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.*
>
> *Here's what it does:*
> - *It answers patient questions automatically — even at midnight*
> - *It collects the patient's name, phone number, and books them an appointment*
> - *It sends you an instant WhatsApp alert whenever a new patient books*
> - *And you can see all your leads and appointments in a simple dashboard*
>
> *So your staff doesn't have to answer the same questions 50 times a day. The AI handles it. And you never miss a lead again."*

---

## ─── STEP 4: OFFER THE DEMO (30 seconds) ───

> **Goal: Get them excited to see the live demo.**

**Say this:**

> *"We actually have a live demo you can see right now — it's a real dental clinic AI system we built. Can I send you the link on WhatsApp? You can see it working in real-time — the chatbot, the booking, everything.*
>
> *It takes less than 2 minutes to look at and I think you'll really like it."*

---

*(Send them this link on WhatsApp immediately:)*
**`https://dantal-flow.vercel.app/`**

*(Also send the pricing PDF)*

---

## ─── STEP 5: HANDLE OBJECTIONS ───

> **Goal: Address their concerns calmly and confidently.**

---

### Objection 1: "How much does it cost?"

> *"It depends on what you need. Our Starter package starts at ₹15,000 one-time setup — that includes a complete website, booking system, WhatsApp connection, and contact forms. Our Complete AI System is ₹25,000 setup and adds the AI chatbot, CRM dashboard, and full automation.*
>
> *There's also a small monthly maintenance fee of ₹5,000 to ₹7,000 that covers reports, updates, and ongoing optimization.*
>
> *For the number of new patients you can get, it pays for itself very quickly."*

---

### Objection 2: "I already have a website"

> *"That's great! But most clinic websites just show information — they don't actively capture leads or book appointments automatically. What we build is a system that works for you 24/7 and turns visitors into booked patients. We can even check your current website and show you exactly what's missing."*

---

### Objection 3: "I need to think about it"

> *"That's totally understandable. Can I ask — what's the main thing you want to think about? Is it the price, or something else? I want to make sure I've answered all your questions.*
>
> *Meanwhile, let me send you our pricing PDF on WhatsApp so you have all the details in writing. Then we can speak again whenever you're ready — no pressure at all."*

---

### Objection 4: "How do I know it will work?"

> *"That's a great question. You can actually see the live demo right now — it's a real system we built, not a presentation. You can test the chatbot yourself. And if you're not happy after we build your system, we'll work with you until you are.*
>
> *We also send you monthly reports so you can see exactly how many new patients the system is bringing in."*

---

### Objection 5: "I don't understand technology"

> *"You don't need to! That's our job. We handle 100% of the setup, design, and technical work. You only need to give us your clinic's information, photos, and treatment list. After that, everything is done for you. You just log in and see your new patients."*

---

### Objection 6: "Is it safe? What about my patient data?"

> *"Very good question. All data is stored securely using enterprise-grade cloud technology. Patient information is encrypted and only accessible to your clinic. We follow standard data privacy practices used by leading healthcare platforms."*

---

## ─── STEP 6: CLOSE THE CALL ───

> **Goal: Get a commitment — either a demo booking, WhatsApp exchange, or advance payment.**

**Option A — If they're interested:**

> *"That's wonderful. The next step is very simple. We would need just a few details about your clinic — name, services, photos — and we collect 50% of the setup fee to begin. Your system will be live within 10 to 14 days.*
>
> *Would you like to proceed with Offer #1 or Offer #2?"*

---

**Option B — If they want more time:**

> *"Absolutely no problem. I'll send you the pricing PDF and demo link on WhatsApp right now so you have everything in writing. When is a good time for us to speak again? I'll follow up then."*

---

**Option C — If they said no to everything:**

> *"Thank you for your time! I'll send you our information on WhatsApp anyway — just in case it's useful in the future. If you ever have questions, please don't hesitate to reach out. Have a wonderful day!"*
